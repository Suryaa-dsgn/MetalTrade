# Content claims register (internal)

Internal developer/content guidance for Oriental Energy and Minerals Limited.
NOT a public page. Tracks which public claims are approved and which are pending,
so copy stays inside the client's confirmed boundaries. Source of truth: the
client's answers in `Website Content Inputs - Answers in Blue.docx` (Phase 9B).

## Approved / current (safe to show publicly)

- Positioning: a licensed mineral aggregator connecting physical commodity supply
  with vetted corporate and institutional demand.
- Suppliers and buyers are NOT introduced to each other (the company sits between
  them as the aggregator).
- Confirmed public commodity list (12): Gold, Copper (cathode), Lithium,
  Columbite-Tantalite (Coltan), Tin, Lead-Zinc, Manganese, Rare Earth Elements
  (REE), Barite, Bitumen, Iron Ore, Crude Oil. Crude Oil and Bitumen are
  commodities, not metals.
- Supplier process: material type, quantity, location, and documents reviewed;
  engineer inspection of the facility and goods; independent third-party
  inspection and testing when a vetted buyer is in place.
- Buyer inputs: specification, target volume, order frequency, target price,
  delivery destination; requirements confirmed, suitable supply proposed, price
  quote provided.
- Logistics: the company coordinates inspection, freight, and customs at the
  origin airport or port.
- Customs at the destination is the buyer's responsibility.
- Financing is NOT provided.
- Verification: supplier verification, origin documents, independent third-party
  inspection.
- Licence, permit, and company registration numbers are available for
  verification on request from vetted suppliers and buyers (documents are NOT
  posted publicly, because they can be copied).
- Regions: suppliers currently in Africa (open to other regions); buyers are
  industrial companies and financial institutions in Asia and the Middle East.
- Proof point published: "4 years of trade experience" (exact wording).
- Contact is form-first; no public email/phone.

## Do NOT claim publicly

- Licensed miner / mining company / mining operator (the company is an aggregator).
- That a mining licence is expected soon (see Pending below; internal only).
- Suppliers are connected/introduced to buyers directly.
- Financing, or customs at the destination, as company services.
- Warehousing, cargo insurance, or shipment tracking as standard services.
- KYC/KYB, beneficial-ownership, or sanctions screening (not confirmed).
- Association membership or certification badges (none currently).
- Public downloadable licence documents or certification downloads.
- "4 years in business" / "operating for 4 years".
- Vague "global network" claims.
